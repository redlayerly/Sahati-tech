# term | English (optional) | definition
Phishing | | A message or link that impersonates a trusted source to get your password, verification code or card details.
Two-factor authentication | 2FA | A second layer after your password, such as a code from an authenticator app. It blocks access even if your password is stolen.
Social engineering | | Tricking the person instead of hacking the device, using urgency, fear or a claim of official authority.
Ransomware | | Malware that encrypts your files and demands money to unlock them. A separate backup is your most important defense.
Penetration testing | Pentest | An authorized attack that simulates a real attacker to find vulnerabilities and fix them before anyone exploits them.
Red team | | A team that plays the adversary in full, both technically and through people, to test an organization's ability to detect and respond.
Zero trust | | No automatic trust for any user or device, even inside the network. Every request is verified again.
Deepfake | | Audio or video generated by AI that imitates a real person saying or doing things they never said or did.
