This message is a composite of dozens of real messages circulating on WhatsApp and Facebook. Read it as a victim would, then we'll take it apart.

> Congratulations! 🎉 Your number has been selected as one of the winners of the telecom company's anniversary draw. Your prize: a new phone + 500 dinars in credit. To claim it, complete your details within 2 hours only: bit.ly/xxxx

## Line one: the excitement

"Congratulations" and "your number has been selected." The scammer opens with a good feeling, because happy people check less. You never entered any draw, but the message doesn't give you time to remember that.

## Line two: the big name

A well-known company's name lends borrowed trust. Real companies contact you from their official, verified numbers and accounts. They don't announce winners in a message from a personal number.

## Line three: the hourglass

"Within 2 hours only." This is the heart of the trick. Urgency cuts off your most important defense: asking someone else or checking for yourself.

## The link: the door

A shortened link hides the real address. Behind it is usually a page asking for one of three things: your card details "to pay the delivery fee," a verification code sent to your phone, or sharing the message with twenty people "to confirm the prize." That last one is why the message spreads in the first place.

:::warning
If you're asked to pay anything to receive a prize, it's not a prize.
:::

:::action
1. Don't tap links in unexpected prize messages.
2. Check the company's official page directly, not through the link.
3. Don't forward the message. Report it as spam.
:::
